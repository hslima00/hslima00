## 👋 About

Electrical and Computer Engineer at the **Portuguese Air Force**, currently in the **Air C2 Programme Office** (Engineering Directorate). I work at the seam between engineering and procurement for major defence systems — writing technical requirements for radar, GBAD and SAM acquisitions, and integrating tactical data links and simulators into the national Command & Control architecture.

My background is a joint Integrated MSc from the **Portuguese Air Force Academy** and **Instituto Superior Técnico**, with a thesis in computer vision on real-time semantic segmentation. I like problems where a sensor, a network and a deadline all have to agree with each other.

### ✈️ KC-390 Simulator × Link 16
`Air C2 PMO · 2024 — present`

**Outcome:** training capability that mirrors live-network behaviour for Air Force crews.

`Requirements engineering` `V&V` `Technical evaluation`
